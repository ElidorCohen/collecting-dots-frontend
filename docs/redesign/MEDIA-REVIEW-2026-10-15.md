# Collecting Dots supplied media review

Reviewed 15 October 2026. Source: [COLLECTING DOTS WEBSITE](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1HlSzzCo4_PBK1bucvYhF6UQr6JV9MC3Z). This is a planning audit. No site changes, media generation, source edits, sharing changes, or external media uploads were made.

## Coverage and review scope

The five supplied folders contain 50 photographs, seven videos, ten logo/font files, and a 23-page event production brief. The 50 photographs and raster logo variants were visually inspected in labeled contact sheets. All seven videos were inspected through sequences sampled at two-second intervals and selected larger frames; durations, dimensions, and codecs were read from the local files. The brief was read as extracted text and rendered page sheets. Soundtracks were not audited. Original assets were retrieved into a temporary review directory; the Drive organization remains unchanged.

The [source inventory](drive-media-inventory-2026-10-15.json) records file links, folder membership, sizes, image dimensions, and video metadata. It excludes temporary authenticated download URLs.

## What the material establishes

This is a useful editorial handoff rather than a random image dump: crowd scenes, performers, signage, lights, a physical brand lightbox, and day/night venue views provide different scales and rhythms for the page.

The event brief makes the identity more specific. Page 2 describes a square-and-dot system, modular design, rhythm, and evolving cover treatments. Pages 9–16 establish custom traffic lights, street signs, stage signage, and chevrons as physical parts of the brand. The photographs confirm those elements in use. Preserve the approved circular entrance concept, but develop its material and light from this actual environment. A traffic-light lens or a lightbox-derived aperture could make a stronger reference than an unrelated charcoal wall; this is a proposal to discuss, not an approved replacement storyboard.

Working palette cues from the photographs: charcoal and off-white for structure, with amber/red/green lights and occasional cool blue. Use light colors as accents and chapter cues. Maintain readable text independently of colored images. The existing square-and-dot mark should remain exact.
