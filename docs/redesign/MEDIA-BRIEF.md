# Collecting Dots media brief

Planning draft, 14 October 2026. This is the collection and shot brief for the existing public-site sections. Gather originals first; choose the final compositions and Higgsfield references before generating clips. No application implementation or media generation is part of this pass.

## Delivery standards

Deliver into `/Users/elidorcohen/collectingdots/media-inbox/` using the section folders below. Quantities describe the requested editorial selection, not a requirement to commission new shoots. Send existing material first and mark any gaps.

- Photos: original full-resolution JPEG, PNG, TIFF, or camera originals. Aim for at least 3000 pixels on the long edge; lower resolution originals are still useful to review. Do not upscale before handoff.
- Artwork: original square exports, preferably 3000 × 3000, with artwork intact.
- Video: camera/editor originals; 4K preferred, 1080p acceptable. Retain the original frame rate. Supply clean continuous takes, without added captions, watermarks, transitions, or a music edit when such versions exist.
- Framing: horizontal 16:9 for desktop scenes; separate vertical 9:16 takes or compositions where available. Leave space around key subjects for alternative crops and titles. Do not force every horizontal shot into a vertical crop.
- Audio: retain original camera sound. Optional brand music should be supplied as WAV or the best available original, with the track title and approved excerpt timestamps.
- Provenance: include a text note with artist/event/product name, date and location where relevant, photographer/filmmaker credit, source link, permission or usage constraints, preferred crop, and must-use timestamps.

Web derivatives, video compression, poster extraction, color matching, and scroll-friendly encoding come later. Original files stay in the inbox rather than either app's public directory.

## Hero

Folder: `hero/`.

Requested pack: three uninterrupted original clips, each 15–30 seconds, and two high-resolution stills.

1. Wide crowd/venue shot: visible depth, recognisable atmosphere, readable lighting, and some clear space for the title.
2. OMRI or DJ-booth shot: medium/wide framing showing the artist within the room rather than only a tight face shot.
3. Detail shot: hands on equipment, light beams, moving crowd silhouettes, or a physical dot/sign installation.
4. Two stills: the strongest crowd image and a matching image suitable as the initial poster.

Select one continuous 6–10 second portion for the main scene. Additional shots provide options; do not combine all of them into a fast montage. Vertical alternatives are desirable for mobile.

Higgsfield H01 supplies a short abstract aperture entrance. Real footage fills the revealed scene. The circle reveal, title, and logo are composited or animated separately so their shapes remain exact.

## Higgsfield production shortlist

The shot durations below are creative targets. Available output duration, resolution, and reference controls depend on the selected model. Higgsfield provides [dolly, focus-change, through-object, and orbit camera presets](https://higgsfield.ai/camera-controls). A supported [image-to-video model with first/last-frame inputs](https://open.higgsfield.ai/models/alibaba/wan-3.0/image-to-video/api-reference) can help anchor compositions; matching frames and product fidelity still require review.

| ID | Target | References needed | Camera and action | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H01 | 6–8 seconds | Approved material/color reference, aperture start/end compositions, selected real crowd scene for later compositing | Slow forward dolly through a physical circular opening in a textured dark surface; move into a spacious light field; keep the destination clear | Hero entrance, followed by real footage |
| H02 | 5–6 seconds | Sharp original fabric macro, full product reference, desired end crop | Slow lateral move across weave/stitching with one gentle focus change; consistent lighting; no new garment details | Merchandise opening detail |
| H03 | 5–6 seconds | Approved abstract dot composition and opening shot's material/color references | Slow pullback reveals a restrained arrangement of physical points/light apertures, with one empty space; settle into a stable ending | Demo chapter atmosphere and visual bookend |
| H04 optional | 5–6 seconds | Approved blank sleeve/display environment and material references | Lateral dolly through a tactile display space; exact cover art added later | About-to-releases transition if needed |

Prepare one approved still composition per shot before animating. For shots using first/last frames, approve both. Test H01 first, then choose the strongest candidate; produce H02/H03 only once its visual language works. Generate separate 16:9 and 9:16 compositions for selected shots. Retain originals, reference frames, prompt/settings, and chosen exports under each shot ID. Extract a matching still poster from every selected video later.

Generation brief for all shots: one uninterrupted move, physically coherent materials and lighting, no built-in title/URL/logo, no automatic montage, no new soundtrack, a readable beginning and end. Reversible scroll scenes should avoid speech, rapid flicker, and abrupt cuts. The complete sequence is assembled outside the generator; a clean cut or mask is preferable to forcing a difficult AI morph.

The three main generated clips are enough for the initial direction. No generation requests, paid credits, or account changes are needed until the source media and reference frames are reviewed.
